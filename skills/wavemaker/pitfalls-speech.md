# Pitfalls: on-camera speech

Speaking takes and the lines in them. Index: [pitfalls.md](pitfalls.md); narration is in [pitfalls-narration.md](pitfalls-narration.md).

**Lip sync on silent faces, and lines over the wrong mouth.**
- Watch out for: a character's own first-person line laid over a take not generated with it; a satire ad lost its candidate this way. Also two people in one speaking take without a guide: H3 moves every visible mouth (below, "Two speakers sharing one frame", for the route that held).
- Check: `review` `lip_sync` with the cut's `lines` and `characters` (examples.md §6; `speaker_mouth_closed`, `wrong_speaker_mouth_active`, `mouth_active_without_speech`), collected until it is no longer `pending`. `deliver` reviews a file with no review of its exact sha by default (charged; `review: false` turns it off), and its `must_tell` names on-camera lines whose lip sync was not measured; the final message says so.
- Why: the eye checks mouths. In the Oct 11-14 production, six follow-ups on one 25 s short were answered with cutaways, crops, splices and a pasted mouth; each kept the defect. A new speaking take of that shot is what fixed it. A third-person narrator over a non-speaking face is the other honest option.

**Two speakers sharing one frame**. H3 I2V + `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` (20 steps) with the scene's recorded lines as the guide (at most two lines per take, padded with silence; the recording muxed as the track) and the listener's mouth closed in the prompt on every line was fine in all 4 pairs, about 3 credits per second of scene. `ltx25-native-a2v` on the same windows is 3-4x cheaper; it held on a short close indoor two-shot, not on a longer exterior with head movement. Ref2VA re-stages the room. Known issues: `edit split_lines` boundaries (`listen` each cut line), and 175-frame windows can pass the H3 300 s bound (600 s). Routes, prompt and costs: [dialogue-scene-craft](wm://card/dialogue-scene-craft).

**Speech guides and the mouth after the line.** Unpadded guides invented words after the line in 4 of 8 takes. Padded with silence to the take length, the 2 takes re-rendered said the line and rested, judged by eye; whether padding keeps the mouth still over the tail, the lead and pauses inside a line has not been measured across takes, and a rom-com showed mouths re-opening after lines. Check each speaking take: the transcript against the line (`listen` with `expected_text`: extra words mean trim or retake, and an unclear transcript is not a pass), `review` `lip_sync` with its `lines` (a mouth moving with no word heard comes back as `mouth_active_without_speech` with its `position`, such as `after_line` or `inside_pause`), and the frames after the last word.

**Pauses the voice model adds**. `eleven_v3` read ellipses and one-word sentences as 0.5-1.7 s silences inside lines; in a speaking take that is a beat the mouth must rest through, and H3 tends to fill unguided time. Check each rendered line's word times with `listen` before making takes; keep a pause only where you want the beat ([dialogue-scene-craft](wm://card/dialogue-scene-craft)).

**Muted takes that keep talking**.
- Watch out for: a speaking take muted under its line. H3 kept generating speech and articulation after the guide ended (speech-level audio 0.6-0.8 s after it, some almost at once), and the mouths moved with it; muted, the cut's soundtrack was clean, so no audio check saw it. Muting also dropped the take's own sound effects: the slap was 13-21 dB under the dialogue, and the final message still promised it.
- Check: the take's `speech` facts (`speech_after_guide_s`, `undeclared_speech`), `compose`'s `editorial_facts` (`clip_audio` lists muted takes whose own audio sounds), the frames after each line's last word, and `listen` to a take before muting it. Keep the take's audio where it carries an effect, or add the effect as an `sfx` cue.
- Why: the eye sees a mouth moving with nothing heard; the ear misses an effect the story needs.

**Silent takes that talk**. A take made without a line can still voice words: s15, told "does not speak", mumbled at 2.3-3.3 s with the mouth moving, stayed in at videoVolume 0.35, and the final message called all speech voice-over. Check the take's `speech` facts (`declared: none` with `undeclared_speech`) and `compose`'s `take_speech_kept` / `take_speech_muted` findings (`clip_audio.speaking`); a scene's `speechMode` (`on-screen`, `off-screen`, `silent`) tells compose which takes may talk. Mute the take, move `sourceStartS` to the voice-free stretch the finding names, or use another take; muted, the mouth can still move, which `review` `lip_sync` measures.

**Lines longer than the take.** The guide is cropped at the take's end and the rest of the line plays over the next face; `generate` with `validate_only` reports it first (`speech` `truncated_voice_s`), and `compose`'s `speech_timing` marks the scene `speech_crosses_cut`. Split at a pause from `listen` word times, across takes or a chain, or rewrite shorter.

**Faces held after the line**. The edit held speakers 0.4-1.5 s after their last word, where mouths re-opened for 0.2-0.3 s; a held face reads as dubbed. `compose`'s `speech_timing` gives each scene's `held_after_speech_s`. Cut on or near the last word, or carry the line onto the listener's reaction.

**Mouths outside the words, fixed by guesswork**. Seven finals shipped mouths moving 0.25-0.8 s after a line, before it or in its pauses; retakes for two pauses each broke something else. Review each speaking take's `lip_sync` (with the line's `words`): `compose` then lists each mismatch in `mouth_outside_speech` with the trim, slide or cut that removes it, and `mouth_unmeasured` names speaking scenes whose take was never measured. Where no edit fixes it, `latentsync` re-drives the mouth to the line (11-14 credits, about 2.5-3.5 min cold for a 3-9 s line) before you make a new take ([dialogue-scene-craft](wm://card/dialogue-scene-craft)).

**A monologue made as takes from one still.** Takes from one still at one framing snap the speaker back to its pose at every cut. One performance is one `h3-long-take-speech` take (to 39.9 s), continued at a pause ([continuous-monologue](wm://card/continuous-monologue)); meant cuts change the framing. InfiniteTalk and S2V stop near 20 s a take.

**A listener during off-screen speech**. H3 moves a listening face's mouth even when told "lips closed", and the fix there, a hand over his mouth twice, read as shock or nausea and jumped between shots. Frame the listener so the mouth isn't the subject: the back of the head or an over-the-shoulder, a wider shot, or an insert of what he's looking at (the phone screen with the call timer, the interface he's arguing with). Never cover the mouth.

**An emotional switch inside one sentence**. Tears snapping into a glossy announcer was split across two setups, so the switch never happened on camera, the pivot word landed on the wrong shot, and the voice changed only in level (+7 dB, 240 to 250 Hz). Render the two halves as two lines with different direction (tearful, then the announcer: another stability and style, or an announcer voice designed on the same timbre) and check the word on each side of the split. The reliable picture is a hard cut on the same axis, a step tighter, into ad lighting, with a sting on the cut. One FL2VA take from a crying still to a commercial-smile still under one guide is untested.
