## Sound and music

### Use when

Choosing or reviewing speech, score, effects, ambience and the delivered mix.

### Techniques and tradeoffs

Sound can supply location, pace, emotion and off-screen information. Music may support, counterpoint or intentionally leave room for performance. Effects on the actions that matter make generated footage feel real; leave an action silent on purpose. Protect intelligibility through level, spectral space and selective silence. Use measured recording duration and trims for timing. Source audio roles are deliberate keep/mute/replace choices; unreviewed engine audio does not establish an instrumental track. Loudness and silence measurements are useful observations, not listening judgments. Exact words, voice and sync require actual playback evidence.

### Questions to inspect

Can the intended words be heard? Are transitions, room tone and joins convincing? Does music serve rather than mask a weak beat? Are requested silence and intentional sounds preserved? Was actual encoded audio supplied and confirmed for review? Does final playback match declared audio roles?
