## Dialogue

### Use when

Writing or assessing character speech, interviews or exchanges.

### Techniques and tradeoffs

Give speakers a purpose and a relationship to the listener. Vocabulary, rhythm, interruptions and what remains unsaid can distinguish voices. Natural speech can be brief or sustained, and several speakers can share a scene; within one generated H3 take, guide one speaker at a time, because the model invents words for a second voice. Keep acting directions separate from spoken text. Read or listen for intelligibility, emphasis and breath. Word-per-second estimates help plan recording but actual selected performances determine duration. Visual coverage can change while the same thought continues. Lip sync reads only when the speaking mouth is large in frame.

### Questions to inspect

Do voices feel distinct without labels? Does speech pursue a purpose? Are lines understandable in context? Are exact words, speaker and selected voice preserved? Does measured recording fit the edit? Does requested visible speech actually synchronize?
