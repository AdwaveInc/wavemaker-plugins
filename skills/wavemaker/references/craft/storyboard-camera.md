## Storyboard and camera

### Use when

Selecting shot coverage, motion, spatial relationships or endpoint images.

### Techniques and tradeoffs

Choose viewpoint, distance and camera motion for what needs to be understood or felt. Establish geography when it matters; inserts can clarify an action, while close framing can emphasize reaction. Continuity of eyelines, screen direction and object state helps edits read; deliberate discontinuity can instead express disorientation or a changed viewpoint. Entrances, exits and action/reaction coverage can connect a sequence. A board may describe edited dialogue, continuous action, existing footage or graphics without prescribing one move per beat. For generated shots, reviewed entry and landing images can clarify difficult transitions; inspect the selected node’s actual consumed endpoints. Identity plates support conditioning but do not by themselves establish the intended shot.

### Questions to inspect

Is the important action readable? Does framing serve the emotion or information? Are camera and subject motion compatible? Can the next cut connect in space and state? Do endpoint images show the required change? Are performance details actually visible?
