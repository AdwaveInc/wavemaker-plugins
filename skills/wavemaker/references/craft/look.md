## Look and image fidelity

### Use when

Creating or inspecting images, light, materials, color and recognizable subjects.

### Techniques and tradeoffs

Control hierarchy through light, contrast, focus, scale and color relationships. Motivated lighting can ground a scene; stylized light can serve another intent. Material response, contact shadows and proportions affect credibility. Preserve supplied recognizable product/subject features and compare actual output against originals. Aspect conversion can crop or contain; squeezing geometry changes identity. A coherent palette or texture can connect scenes, but local variation may support story. References communicate desired appearance; binding receipts are not proof of fidelity. A photo reads real when the prompt describes the capture (camera, lens, framing) and the scene's actual light; cinematic, photoreal or 8k push it glossy.

### Questions to inspect

What draws attention first? Are face, product details, markings and proportions faithful? Do materials and lighting make sense for the chosen style? Are crops deliberate? Does color support hierarchy and continuity? Does the full-size image hold up beyond a thumbnail?
