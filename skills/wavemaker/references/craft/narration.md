## Narration

### Use when

Voiceover, explanation, news, presentations or music-led spoken structure.

### Techniques and tradeoffs

Narration can orient, argue, contrast with picture or connect information across cuts. Avoid needless duplication of what the image already explains. Choose tone for audience and source; fact-bound formats cannot invent claims. Silence and uninterrupted speech are both useful. Phrase lengths, pauses and emphasis should come from the performance and meaning, not a fixed cut grid. Estimated word capacity is provisional until recording. Declare who speaks each line in the outline (a character on camera, a narrator or nobody), follow it in prompts and the mix, and update it with a one-line reason when you change course.

### Questions to inspect

Does each passage add useful meaning? Are claims grounded? Can listeners follow without seeing every detail? Are joins and source offsets clean? Are full approved words audible? Does picture emphasis follow or intentionally contrast the narration?
