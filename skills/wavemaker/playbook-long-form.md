## Long form (documentary, episode, explainer, over about 2 min)

**What usually works.** Segments of 60-150 s, each planned, generated and rendered on its own, then concatenated into a master. Narration first, a rough cut with placeholders, upgrades in place; a full-length draft early, replaced later. Voiceover by default; interviews as lip-synced talking heads; B-roll every 4-8 s; archive stills with a designed move up to about 8 s. A shot ledger per segment with status for every shot. Stems for films over 2 min: dialogue -20, effects -26, score -30 to -23 LUFS, master -14.

**Graphs and pathways.** `pathway:feature-film`, `pathway:h3-chains`, `pathway:h3-context` (see [examples.md](examples.md)).

**Pitfalls.** A narrated still held long reads as a slideshow (a 12-minute documentary was 3% motion). Responses have an output cap: long files go better in parts (about 300 lines), tool calls under about 5k tokens.

**Questions to ask of the cut.**
- Is every segment rendered and in the master, with runtime inside the brief?
- Does the picture keep moving with the narration; is each held still a deliberate beat?
- Is narration complete and intelligible, with music ducked under it?
