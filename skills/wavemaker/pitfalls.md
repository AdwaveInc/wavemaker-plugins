# Pitfalls

They are in short files, one per stage, each small enough to read whole. Codes in backticks are what the checks may report. Tools: `read wm://capability/<id>` ([capabilities.md](capabilities.md) lists them); patterns: [examples.md](examples.md).

| File | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [pitfalls-identity.md](pitfalls-identity.md) | People, places and props across shots: one hero keyframe per lead, plates for recurring props, real subjects, continuity at cuts, geography, small faces, contact sheets |
| [pitfalls-motion.md](pitfalls-motion.md) | Takes that move: "slowly" prompts, slowed playback, reversed travel, lettering in motion, fast elements, morphing, chains, re-camera |
| [pitfalls-stills.md](pitfalls-stills.md) | Image models and edits: undress by omission, prompt words that become defects, restage leaks, packaging, texture, masks; text on screen |
| [pitfalls-speech.md](pitfalls-speech.md) | On-camera lines: lip sync, speech guides, pauses, muted takes, lines longer than the take, faces held after the line |
| [pitfalls-narration.md](pitfalls-narration.md) | Voiceover and voices: one continuous read, short lines, flat character reads, casting notes, timing, lines written for the eye, voice drift, take audio, loudness |
| [pitfalls-delivery.md](pitfalls-delivery.md) | The edit, review and delivery: delivery size, waived defects, pending sections, runtime, request ids, slideshows, wide and vertical parity, feed safe zones, the final message |

The rules most often skipped are also in the playbook steps where they apply ([playbooks.md](playbooks.md) "Ad or TV spot" and "Narrative short or trailer").
