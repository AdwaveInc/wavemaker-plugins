## Social short and UGC vertical

**What usually works.** Usually 9:16 with the subject centred, head in the upper third and the lower band clear of feed captions. Social: hook in the first second, payoff early (feeds lose viewers after about 15 s), then a loop or CTA; 15-60 s, a single gag or dance near 15 s; shots 1-2.5 s; cuts on the beat (bar = 240 / BPM seconds). A named routine (dance, trick, recipe) satisfies when seen through, each step covered (full-body wides plus cut-ins). UGC and hauls: the creator's opinion is the content, so they talk on camera about every item from the first second; 20-45 s; shots 2-3 s (edit rhythm: a creator talking to the phone is usually one performance, a long take continued at a pause, with punch-ins and cutaways over it, [continuous-monologue](wm://card/continuous-monologue)); handheld, available light, a real room. On-camera word budget is roughly floor((seconds - 0.9) x 2.2). An anticipation hook, 2-4 s of something about to happen cut before it does, made fresh with the turn's video model and tied to the subject, can open a short or go in front of a video the user brings ([anticipation-hook](wm://card/anticipation-hook)). One locked-off take the user brings can be cut between angles: punch-ins first, a re-camera or a reference-video re-shoot only for angles a crop cannot make, the take's own sound underneath ([coverage-from-one-take](wm://card/coverage-from-one-take)).

**Graphs and pathways.** Speaking takes ([examples.md](examples.md) §7); `pathway:motion-transfer` for a dance from a real clip; `pathway:ad-recipes` (try-on selfie, review in car, reply-to-comment).

A short that tells a scripted story with characters (a micro-thriller, a sketch) is also a narrative: its script, plates, sets and coverage follow the narrative stages ([playbook-narrative.md](playbook-narrative.md)).

**Pitfalls.** One 10 s take as the whole piece leaves no edit rhythm (a dancing gorilla); a silent haul over music loses what viewers come for. Outfit changes read best on cuts, each look from its own keyframe built from the creator's plate; see morphing in [pitfalls-motion.md](pitfalls-motion.md).

**Questions to ask of the cut.**
- Would you stop scrolling in the first second?
- Is the named action complete on screen?
- Is it the same creator (face, body, voice) throughout, with no second person appearing?
- Does the creator speak about every item, matching the script?
- Does the end loop or land on a hit?
